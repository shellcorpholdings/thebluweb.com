About ShellCorp

The institution
behind the brands.

ShellCorp Holdings Inc. is a Barbados-based digital holding company built to incubate, grow, and scale technology-driven ventures across sectors that define the modern Caribbean economy. We operate with the discipline of an institution and the conviction of a company that knows exactly what it's building.

Our portfolio spans eCommerce marketplaces, RWA tokenisation, and property auctions — three verticals with transformative growth potential across the Caribbean, Latin America, and beyond. Two of our three ventures operate at the convergence of Caribbean real estate and digital innovation — by design. Our mission is deliberate: to create interconnected digital infrastructure that keeps economic ownership rooted in the region.

"Born in the Caribbean.
Built for the world."

ShellCorp Holdings provides each subsidiary with governance, capital strategy, and operational infrastructure — while preserving the independence and identity that makes each brand exceptional. One parent. Three market leaders.

ShellCorp Holdings was founded with a clear conviction: that the Caribbean is not just a market to be served — it is a launchpad for enterprises that can compete and win on the world stage.

The company is led by its founder, whose vision drives every strategic decision across the portfolio — from the ventures we build to the markets we choose to define.

Shelley Shepherd
Founder & Chief Executive
ShellCorp Holdings Inc.
Shelley Shepherd is the Founder & CEO of ShellCorp Holdings Inc., a Barbados-based digital holding company building the Caribbean's next generation of technology-driven ventures. Her career spans over 15 years across multiple sectors — construction, decorative concrete, real estate, property management, distribution, pharmaceutical, music, and printing — giving her a rare, cross-industry lens on how Caribbean businesses operate and where digital transformation creates the most value. That breadth, combined with deep experience in AI strategy and digital platforms, is the foundation of ShellCorp's portfolio. The Blu Web — the Caribbean's first AI-powered Real World Asset advisory — is the natural extension of that work: bringing clarity, transparency, and liquidity to Caribbean assets that have always deserved them.
